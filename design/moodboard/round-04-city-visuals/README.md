# Round 04 — illustrative city skins

**Status:** superseded by round 05. Kept as history.

This round tried four palette/detail passes over the current city plate:
Illustrated Quarter (late afternoon), Blue Hour (night), Paper Diorama (tactile)
and Rain & Neon (wet night). The intent was to lift the basic block city without
changing the board.

The director's reaction was that the results still looked “pretty much like a
children's drawing”; the target is a much denser, RTS-style city rather than a
pastel illustration. Round 05 replaced this direction with the RTS skins.

The code remains available in the `?visuals` lab as a comparison, but none of
these themes is in the game's skin selector.
