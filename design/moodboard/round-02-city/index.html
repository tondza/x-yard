All four mockups render the same city and the same game state — only art style and perspective change — so this is a controlled comparison, not four unrelated proposals.

What is identical in all four

Deliberately held constant so only style and perspective vary:
  • The same fictional city, Yard-Meridian — same river, park, bridges, cathedral square, Central Station, South Terminal and stadium.
  • The same 24 numbered stations at the same coordinates.
  • The same game state: turn 6 of 24, pulse fired T3 and next at T8; F1 revealed at 104, F2 hidden since T3 (last known 102), F3 hidden with heavy contraband near 112.
  • The same probability spread for F2: 102 40%, 111 25%, 103 20%, 105 15%.
  • The same five ticket-log rows, including one Unknown Transport.
  • The same HUD content and the same 1280×720 stage.

What to decide

  1. Perspective — pure top-down (E, H) or slight 2.5D tilt (F, G)? Compare E against F directly: they are the same style, so any difference you feel is purely the camera.
  2. Art style — flat vector (E, F), pixel-art (G), or painterly (H)? Compare F against G, which share a perspective.
  3. Day or night as the default? H is night and looks the most atmospheric; E/F/G are day and read more easily. This is really a question about the game's tone.
  4. How much illustration before legibility suffers? G is the busiest, E the calmest. Which side of that line do you want as the default, given players will be reading it under time pressure?

Already decided (do not re-open here)

Recorded in VISUAL_DIRECTION.md: hybrid route display (route lines appear only for the selected character's legal moves); on-foot movement is node-to-node along street edges and free; ~600–900 stations per ~100-player city, procedurally generated then hand-tuned; hidden Fugitives are viewer-dependent; SimCity rooftops and palette plus a day-night feel, but no 1990s UI chrome; ambient pedestrians and cars deliberately minimal; animation priority is characters then day-night; fictional cities inspired by real ones; and mockups must show both a zoomed view and a full-city overview.

Known gaps

These are directional sketches. Still open: mobile and small viewports, accessibility (contrast and colour-blind safety for the mode colours), hover/focus/disabled states, motion-design budget, the lobby and matchmaking flow, the end-of-Yard summary, spectating, and how the renderer actually behaves at ~618 stations with ~100 players — the overview insets assert that scale but do not prove it.
