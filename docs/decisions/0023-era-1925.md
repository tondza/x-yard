# 0023 — Era moved to 1925 (interwar)

**Status:** accepted — 2026-11-11

**Supersedes:** [0019](0019-1907-era-rules.md) in its era framing only. The
no-motor-scooter and keep-special decisions in 0019 still stand.

## Context

The round-07 visual review exposed an anachronism. The prototype's paid mode is
**taxi**, but in 1907 motor taxis were a novelty in most cities; the street was
still dominated by horses and early omnibuses. The director's feedback was direct:

> "1907 is definitely not the correct era. Should be later, since in 1907 taxis
> were not prominent."

The city art (gas lamps, hansom cabs, early motor omnibuses, Edwardian and
interwar civic buildings) reads correctly later in the early motor age. None of
the movement rules depend on the calendar.

## Decision

The presentation era is **1925 (interwar)**. Player-facing period copy, skin
kickers and living documents say 1925. The scooter stays removed, `special`
stays, and the internal mode names (`taxi` / `bus` / `underground` / `special`)
do not change. Landmarks remain period-plausible: no neon, billboards, air
conditioning or antennas, and motor traffic is present but sparse.

## Rejected alternatives

1. **Keep 1907 and relabel the taxi mode.** Rejected: it would rename a
   wire/rules mode for a presentation fix, and 1907 still makes a prominent motor
   taxi network anachronistic.
2. **Move to the present day.** Rejected: it destroys the Vintage Etching
   identity and the deliberate early-motor-age mood.
3. **Change the rules numbers with the era.** Rejected: the era is presentation.
   The ticket economy, movement modes, graph and turn structure are untouched;
   this is not a rules round.
4. **Keep every historical document saying 1907.** Rejected for *living* docs
   (status, visual direction, plan, brief, skin copy); historical records and old
   `PROJECT_LOG.md` entries are left as written, because they were true when
   written.

## Consequences

- 0019's era framing is superseded; its no-scooter and keep-special decisions
  remain in force.
- The Vintage Etching skin now reads "1925". Gas lamps, hansom cabs and early
  motor traffic coexist, which is accurate for the period.
- A future era change needs a new record; past log entries that say 1907 are
  history and must not be rewritten.
