# 0019 — 1907 era rules: no motor-scooter; special stays

**Status:** accepted — 2026-10-29

## Context

The director chose the **early 1900s / isometric Vintage Etching** direction:
late enough for the Underground to exist, early enough that the modern
motor-scooter no longer belongs. The earlier six-option prototype had already
shown the scooter was the most complex movement option and the least necessary
for the deduction loop. The instruction was explicit:

> “Let's go for the isometric vintage skin and apply the era rules as well (no
> scooter, special can be kept).”

This is a rules change, not only a skin change.

## Decision

1. **Remove the motor-scooter from the era rules.** `MobilityOption` is
   `TransportMode | 'foot'`; `LoggedTransport` no longer contains `scooter`; the
   prototype no longer has `scooterCharges`, a scooter move command, a scooter
   log row, a `RevealReason: 'scooter'`, or the scooter-reveal playback step.
2. **Keep the special link.** `special` remains a paid, street-transcending
   option. The era rules have five choices: taxi, bus, underground, special and
   foot.
3. **Do not rename the paid modes yet.** The internal vocabulary stays
   `taxi` / `bus` / `underground` / `special`; the vintage presentation may use
   period labels, but no wire or rules rename is bundled into this change.
4. **The isometric Vintage Etching skin becomes the default city skin.** Basic
   remains the fallback and is still selectable. Iron and Night remain developer
   skins.
5. **The full-game `TransportMode` stays unchanged.** It never contained
   `foot` or `scooter`; this decision only removes the prototype's extra.

## Rejected alternatives

1. **Keep the scooter but hide it in the vintage HUD.** Rejected: the director
   asked for era rules; a modern motor-scooter in a 1907 map would be a lie in
   the interface, not just an art mismatch.
2. **Remove special too.** Rejected: special is an established transit tier and
   the director explicitly said it can stay; the special link is period-plausible
   as a service passage.
3. **Rename every mode while removing the scooter.** Rejected for this change:
   `taxi`/`bus`/`underground` are wire and edge vocabulary; renaming them touches
   the full-game protocol, tests and historical docs for no gameplay gain. A
   presentation-only era label is the safer follow-up if wanted.
4. **Keep the scooter as a full-game-only option.** Rejected: it would recreate
   the removed prototype complexity later without a current owner or use.

## Consequences

- The prototype kit has five modes; the rules reminder and legal-move list no
  longer mention the scooter.
- `SPEC`/historical MVP documents that still describe the scooter are superseded
  in that respect by this record; they remain as history.
- The full suite stays at **7 files / 131 tests**; the prototype suite drops the
  scooter-specific assertion rather than adding a new mode.
- The isometric Vintage skin, its projected overlay/camera and the paper HUD are
  the default presentation; Basic is the fallback.
